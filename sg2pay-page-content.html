Questions

How would I pay for something with silver?

Add the items you hold to your SG2Pay wallet, enter a price, and SG2Pay shows which items to hand over. It gives the closest combination below and above the price, so both sides can agree on change.

Can I trade silver for gold, or for copper?

Yes. Any metal can be the currency a price is stated in, and SG2Pay converts between gold, silver and copper using current or agreed prices.

What if there are no live prices?

Enter spot prices manually. SG2Pay uses whatever prices you set, so it still works when markets or price feeds are unavailable.

Does SG2Pay buy, sell or hold metal?

No. It's a free calculator. It never handles money or metal, and there's no account to create.

SG2Pay is a calculator. It does not buy, sell, store or transfer metal, and nothing in it is investment advice.
